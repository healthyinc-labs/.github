> Healthy Research Labs (`@healthyinc-labs`) operates as the dedicated scientific research, clinical validation, and advanced exploratory engineering division of **[Healthy Inc.](https://github.com/healthyinc)**. All intellectual property, federal grant initiatives (United States National Science Foundation and National Institutes of Health SBIR/STTR), reinforcement learning frameworks, and empirical clinical benchmarks hosted in this organization are governed under corporate stewardship by Healthy Inc.

## Ecosystem Architectural Division

To maintain strict regulatory boundaries, institutional data governance, and production security, the Healthy ecosystem enforces an absolute structural separation between corporate commercial systems and exploratory research:

| Institutional Pillar | Organization | Core Scope & Responsibilities | Key Technologies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial Operations & Production Infrastructure** | **[Healthy Inc. (`@healthyinc`)](https://github.com/healthyinc)** | Commercial SuperApp production codebases (`App-MVP`), Habitcoin decentralized tokenomic protocol, Solana/Eclipse smart contract infrastructure, and enterprise hospital deployment pipelines. | React Native, Solana SVM, Rust, Eclipse SVM, EVM Sepolia, AWS Nitro Enclaves |
| **Scientific R&D & Federal Grants** | **Healthy Research Labs (`@healthyinc-labs`)** | United States federal grant initiatives (NSF/NIH), hospital reinforcement learning simulations, multi-agent Care Traffic Control prototypes, and clinical data science benchmarks. | Python 3.12, PyTorch, DuckDB, MIMIC-III/IV, PhysioNet DUA, PettingZoo |
